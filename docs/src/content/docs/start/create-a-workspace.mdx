<Steps>

1. **Go to [app.glacis.dev](https://app.glacis.dev).**

   The sign-in page has a **Create account** panel underneath it. Work email
   and a password of at least 8 characters is the whole form.

   <Screenshot shape="phone" caption="app.glacis.dev — sign in, with Create account below it." />

2. **Check your email.**

   Signup does not sign you in. A verification link is sent first, and the
   account is inert until you click it. If the mail has not arrived in a few
   minutes, check spam and then request a new link from the same page.

   <Aside type="note">
     The signup response is deliberately identical whether or not the address
     already has an account. It tells you a link is on its way either way, so
     the form cannot be used to test whether someone has an account here.
   </Aside>

3. **Sign in.**

   Your **personal workspace** already exists — it was created with the
   account. You can add or join a company workspace afterwards from inside the
   product.

4. **Answer the profile.**

   A handful of questions: what you build, which model providers you use, how
   it is deployed, whether regulated data is involved, roughly how big the
   team is. It shapes what the workspace shows you next. Skip it if you would
   rather just look around.

</Steps>

## What you get

- A workspace of your own, with sample receipts already in it — see
  [The sample workspace](/start/sample-workspace/).
- The ability to mint a receipt from the browser — see
  [Mint a receipt](/start/mint-a-receipt/).
- Workspace API keys (`glsk_…`) when you are ready to connect a real system —
  see [Connect](/connect/).

## What it costs

Nothing to start, and there is no card in this flow.

Self-signed receipts from the SDK are free forever and do not involve us at
all — they are produced entirely inside your own process. Witnessed receipts
have a free tier. **Receipts are not metered.** Any rate limit you meet is
abuse control, not billing.

<Aside type="caution" title="This is not a compliance product">
  Creating a workspace does not make anything compliant, protected, or
  certified, and neither does a receipt. A receipt is evidence that a claim was
  generated, verifiable by someone who does not trust you. What that evidence
  is worth in a given regime is a judgement for the person reading it — see
  [what a check proves](/verify/what-a-check-proves/).
</Aside>
