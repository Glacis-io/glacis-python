You do not need to install anything to see what a GLACIS receipt is and check
one for yourself. Three steps, all in a browser, all of it fine on a phone.

<Steps>

1. **[Create a workspace](/start/create-a-workspace/)** — email and a password at
   [app.glacis.dev](https://app.glacis.dev). About a minute, including the
   verification email.

2. **[Look at the sample receipts](/start/sample-workspace/)** — your new workspace
   is not empty. It comes with synthetic receipts, every one badged
   <ReceiptBadge kind="sample" />, so you can see the shape of the thing before
   you produce one.

3. **[Mint a real one](/start/mint-a-receipt/)** — describe an event, get back a
   receipt bound to your workspace, and check it yourself.

</Steps>

Then, when you want receipts to come out of your own system instead of a form,
[Connect](/connect/) is the ten-minute version: a config block or three lines of
Python.

## What a receipt is

A receipt is a signed, tamper-evident record that a specific AI operation
happened and that specific controls ran around it. It carries hashes of the
input and output, not the input and output — so it can be handed to someone
outside your organisation without handing over the data.

A receipt is **evidence a claim was generated**. It is not a compliance
certificate, it is not an audit result, and it does not say the model was right.
[What a green check does and does not establish](/verify/what-a-check-proves/)
is worth three minutes of your time before you show one to anybody.

## The three states, and why they are never mixed

<div class="badge-legend">

| State | Meaning | What you can do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <ReceiptBadge kind="sample" /> | Synthetic. No inference happened. | Learn the format. Nothing else. |
| <ReceiptBadge kind="self-signed" /> | You signed your own record. | Internal record-keeping. It is a diary. |
| <ReceiptBadge kind="witnessed" /> | Countersigned by a party that did not author it. | Hand to a third party as evidence. |

</div>

A self-signed receipt is not a weaker witnessed receipt. It is a different
claim: *"I say this happened."* A witnessed receipt says *"I say this happened,
and someone who is not me recorded that I said it, at a time I could not choose
after the fact."* Everything in these docs keeps those two apart.
