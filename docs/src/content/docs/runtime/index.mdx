The Glacis **runtime product** sits in front of the actions your AI takes.
Covered decisions are evaluated while the action is live (allow / constrain /
hold / deny), and each covered decision can leave a **signed record** someone
else can check — without Glacis taking custody of prompts or outputs.

This page is operator documentation for buyers and deployers. Marketing
orientation lives on [glacis.io/platform](https://www.glacis.io/platform).

<Aside type="caution" title="Approved assurance claim (do not exceed)">
  For the **customer-hosted bundle** (proxy + Notary + dashboard) in its default
  configuration, Glacis stands behind an **OVERT Level 1 Core** evidence posture
  at **Attestation Assurance Level AAL-3**. A single operator-controlled,
  co-resident Notary caps the deployment at AAL-3. Glacis is **not** independently
  certified and must **not** be represented as AAL-4, "evidence-grade," EU AI Act
  certified, ISO certified, or SOC attested. See the internal `CLAIM.md` in labs
  (`mvp-product/CLAIM.md`) — public docs paraphrase it; they do not expand it.
</Aside>

## What it does today

- **Hold actions to bounds** — policy evaluation on the request path for governed
  traffic (mediated scope). Records attest what was governed and are silent about
  un-mediated paths.
- **Emit signed records** — Ed25519 over canonical bytes for covered decisions;
  verification can run in the customer environment.
- **Distinguish status honestly** — enforced, recorded, declared-not-enforced, or
  not-present. Absence of a detector is reported; it is not sold as protection.
- **Zero content egress by construction** — records carry hashes and metadata,
  not raw prompts or responses. Provider keys stay with the customer.

## Mediated scope (canonical coverage claim)

> For governed paths, Glacis produces independently verifiable evidence of policy
> execution. Records attest what was governed and are silent about un-mediated paths.

Glacis governs AI **transactions** in a mediated scope — it is not a universal
packet-capture layer for all AI traffic. Streaming is observe/truncate, never
"prevention" of already-emitted tokens. Provider breadth for drop-in ingress is
**OpenAI-compatible governed paths** unless a contract expands it.

## What this product is not

- Not a compliance certificate, safety guarantee, or "Utah-certified" anything.
- Not a replacement for your GRC platform, SIEM, or identity stack.
- Not EU AI Act / ISO 42001 / SOC 2 "done" by installing the bundle.
- Not open-source enforcement IP — the policy language may be open; the
  commercial runtime is not. Regulated buyers can [inspect under NDA](/runtime/inspect-under-nda/).
